## ✨ Project Overview

Ding redefines digital interaction by combining traditional networking with advanced AI capabilities. We are building a digital world where technology adapts to your feelings.

### Noro: Your AI Companion
At the heart of Ding lies **Noro**, an emotionally intelligent AI:
* **Mood Analysis:** Interacts via voice and facial recognition to offer empathy.
* **Adaptive Feeds:** Content that changes based on your mental state.
* **Safety First:** Proactively connects users to support when distress is detected.

### Immersive 3D Experience
The frontend is an interactive 3D space. Using **Three.js** and **React Three Fiber (R3F)**, we create fluid, high-performance environments that house the Noro AI and interactive social components.

## 🔭 Vision

Ultimately, Ding aims to create a human-centered digital world — one where technology adapts to emotions, connections are authentic, and users feel safe, understood, and empowered.

By merging AI companionship, creative expression, and community engagement, Ding represents the next evolution of social networking — a smarter, safer, and more emotionally aware digital society.
